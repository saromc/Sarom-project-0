## Training Log

A private, single-user fitness planner in one file: `index.html`.

**Where to use it:** the main copy lives as a private page on claude.ai. There, entries are saved to your Claude account and the "Add from Claude" box can read pasted replies and screenshots. Opening `index.html` directly from this repository also works, but it saves only in that browser and can't read text or screenshots for you.

**Tabs**
- **Today** – calorie ring and macro bars against your targets, volume lifted, body weight, workouts this week, logging streak, a front-and-back body map of muscles hit this week, and a shortcut to add food from a screenshot.
- **Food** – add food from a screenshot (read automatically, checked before saving) or by hand, with calories, protein, carbs and fat; one-tap re-add of recent foods.
- **Gym** – exercises picked by category (Chest, Back, Legs, Shoulders, Arms, Other), including leg extension, seated leg curls, hip abduction and hip adduction under Legs, with sets, reps and weight in lb; per-exercise history chart.
- **Plan** – a Monday–Sunday training template that becomes a checklist on Today.
- **Body** – body weight (lb) and waist (in) with a trend chart.
- **Progress** – month and year results: days logged, workouts, average calories and protein, volume lifted, weight change, charts, muscle map and per-lift progress.
- **Settings** (gear icon) – daily calorie and macro targets, backup export/import (JSON), CSV export, erase.

Units: pounds and inches for training and body measurements; grams and kcal for food.

The body map shades each muscle by sets per week on a fixed scale (blue for a few, red for 20 or more). Exercise-to-muscle assignments are based on published EMG studies; counting weekly sets, with half credit for assisting muscles, follows the research on training volume and muscle growth.
